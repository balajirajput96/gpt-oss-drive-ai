# Reel 0018 Research — Do Experts Disagree?

## Deterministic scope

**Tuple:** `MND/L01/Q18`  
**Question:** क्या विशेषज्ञों में मतभेद है?  
**Topic:** scientific disagreement in attention and consciousness research can concern mechanisms, definitions, methods, and model scope.

## Evidence boundaries

| Claim | Label | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Attentional capture has active theoretical disagreements, while empirical evidence informs multiple positions. | Review | Disagreement is not proof that “nothing is known.”[^1] |
| Predictive-processing accounts are proposed integrative frameworks; their coverage and mechanisms remain debated. | Theory/review | A framework is not a settled complete explanation of every mental phenomenon.[^1] |
| A consciousness scoping review identified many theories and heterogeneous perspectives. | Scoping review | The review describes theoretical diversity, not a personal consciousness test.[^2] |
| Better disagreement questions specify which result, mechanism, prediction, or measurement would differentiate accounts. | Methodological inference | This is a research practice, not a guaranteed resolution rule. |

## Allowed script framing

The reel may say that experts can agree on some observed patterns while disagreeing about why they occur, which model generalizes, or what a measure captures. It must not portray disagreement as evidence-free confusion, assert that one theory has “won,” diagnose a person, or promise a practical intervention.
