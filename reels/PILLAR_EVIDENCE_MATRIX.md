# Per-Pillar Evidence Matrix

This matrix controls what can be drafted before a reel-specific source ledger is approved. The listed sources are **starting anchors**, not permission to copy claims across every topic. Each reel still requires a claim-level source check and must cite the most relevant supporting source in its metadata.

| Pillar | Starting source base | Allowed claim type | Required limitation / prohibited overclaim |
|---|---|---|---|
| MND — Attention, mind, consciousness | Scholarly consciousness reviews and philosophy-of-mind reference works [1] [2] | Definitions, competing theories, and explicitly bounded neural-correlate findings | Never claim a brain scan “proves” subjective experience or that one theory is settled. |
| NBR — Brain, neurons, neuroplasticity | Clinical neuroscience education and human-learning/neuroplasticity reviews [3] [4] | Broad definitions of plasticity and intervention-specific findings | Never promise that a generic habit “rewires” the brain, creates neurons, or changes a named region without direct evidence. |
| MEM — Memory, learning | Retrieval-practice and sleep–memory meta-analytic work [5] [6] | Effects tied to task, study population, and retention interval | Never promise photographic memory or confuse lab-memory effects with universal academic outcomes. |
| SLP — Sleep, recovery, performance | Meta-analyses of experimentally manipulated sleep deprivation [6] | Findings about total acute sleep deprivation and newly learned material | Do not generalize acute total-deprivation effects to every pattern of chronic partial sleep loss; do not give medical sleep treatment. |
| EMO — Emotion regulation | Meta-analysis and systematic review literature on emotion regulation and clinical outcomes [7] | Associations or intervention outcomes with their target population | Do not diagnose anxiety/depression or imply that one strategy works identically in all cultures or contexts. |
| HAB — Habits, motivation, behavior | Habit-formation systematic review and behavior-change intervention reviews [8] [9] | Automaticity, consistent context, and range-based timelines | Never use a universal “21/30/66-day rule” or promise habit success through willpower alone. |
| DEC — Judgment, bias, decision making | Decision-science and cognitive-bias reviews [10] | Definitions, tested bias examples, and debiasing uncertainty | Do not label a viewer as biased from a single example or present bias awareness as a cure. |
| REL — Relationships, communication, social connection | Social-connection reviews and intervention syntheses [11] [12] | Distinctions among isolation, loneliness, and connection; outcome associations | Do not claim social contact alone treats depression, or imply causal effects from cross-sectional evidence. |
| MED — Meditation, self-awareness, contemplation | Controlled-trial syntheses and tradition-specific primary/academic sources [13] | Limited, population-specific outcomes and attributed practice descriptions | Never call a contemplative claim a proven biomedical fact or position meditation as a replacement for treatment. |
| PHS — Philosophy, values, meaning, spirituality | Primary texts, academic philosophy, and reference scholarship [1] [14] | Attributed arguments, concepts, and plural perspectives | Always distinguish philosophical argument or spiritual belief from empirical evidence; do not assert metaphysical claims as tested science. |
