# Reel 0027 Text-Free Visual Storyboard

Six original portrait scenes will use abstract motion-graphics metaphors only: an oversized first pebble at a path entrance; a chair and closed book as a stable-context cue; a fork resolving into one small route; repeated circular cue-to-action marks; contrast of a simple dot path and a layered route; and a calm adjustable dial beside a growing path. No words, numbers, logos, people, legacy files, or generated embedded text may appear. Hindi captions will be composited separately.
