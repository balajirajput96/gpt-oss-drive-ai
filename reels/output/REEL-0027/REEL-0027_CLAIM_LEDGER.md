# Reel 0027 Claim Ledger

| Script idea | Label | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Small change may be a workable start | Conditional behavioural-science framing | Not universal or guaranteed. |
| A clear cue can support repeating an action | Review-supported design principle | No causal brain or health claim. |
| “If–then” plan specifies context and action | Definition from scholarly review | Planning tool, not treatment. |
| Simplicity and context vary by task/person | Evidence-bounded limitation | No fixed timeline or success prediction. |

Excluded: 21-day rule; motivation/health/productivity promise; blame; diagnosis; neural causation; financial or clinical action.
