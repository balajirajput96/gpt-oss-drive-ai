# Reel 0002 Research Record

## Planned title

**क्या ध्यान और चेतना एक ही चीज़ हैं?**

## Evidence-safe core answer

Attention and consciousness are closely related concepts, but they are not interchangeable. A neuroscience review describes overlapping neural activity while treating them as essentially separate processes; this supports cautious language such as “अलग लेकिन जुड़े हुए” rather than a claim that the debate is settled. [1]

Neuroscience often investigates **neural correlates of consciousness**. A correlate is not automatically a complete explanation or proof of causation, and the Stanford reference emphasizes the distinction. [2]
