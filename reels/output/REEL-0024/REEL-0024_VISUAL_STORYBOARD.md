# Reel 0024 Text-Free Visual Storyboard

All six source scenes must be original, text-free, portrait 720×1280 graphics. Hindi captions are a separate final-render overlay. Do not use clinical imagery, real people, logos, helpline numbers, medical icons, legacy assets, or visual diagnosis cues.

| Scene | Time intent | Text-free visual concept | Narrative role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | A soft glowing question mark formed from dots above a balanced path junction. | Need not wait for a label. |
| 2 | Impact lens | Four abstract daily-life tokens—calendar-like grid, book, connected circles, and home outline—surround a central ripple. | Consider everyday impact, not identity. |
| 3 | Professional option | A warm beacon across a bridge toward a neutral doorway; no medical symbols. | Qualified local support is an option. |
| 4 | Different levels | Two separate, equal-height paths: one to a speech-bubble outline, one to a compass-and-doorway outline. | Informal support and professional assessment differ. |
| 5 | Barriers | Semi-transparent knots around a small glowing center dissolve into an open arc. | Shame, privacy/trust, and self-reliance can make help-seeking harder. |
| 6 | First step and safety | A small note-card outline becomes a guiding line toward a bright safe horizon, with an unobtrusive alert ring. | Name the concern, seek local support; immediate danger requires urgent local support. |

## Visual provenance requirements

The generator must record `imageGenerationUsed:false`, `quotaOrAccessControlBypass:false`, `reusedLegacyAsset:false`, `embeddedText:false`, and `realPersonDepicted:false`. SVG source and rendered PNG files must be new Reel-0024-specific artifacts stored outside Git; only the generator source may be committed.
