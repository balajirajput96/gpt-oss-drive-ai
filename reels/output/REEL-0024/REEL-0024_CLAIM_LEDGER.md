# Reel 0024 Claim Ledger

| Script idea | Evidence label | Supporting record | Safe narration boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily-life impact can prompt a support conversation. | Public-health guidance | NIMH, revised 2025 [1] | Say “हो सकता है,” not “ज़रूर है”; do not diagnose. |
| Severe or persistent distress can make professional contact appropriate. | Public-health guidance | NIMH, revised 2025 [1] | No universal two-week rule, treatment direction, or local-service assumption. |
| Informal support and professional assessment are different. | Scope distinction | Research record interpretation | Do not portray a friend as a clinician or imply professionals are required for every concern. |
| Embarrassment, privacy/trust concerns, and self-reliance are reported barriers. | Systematic-review synthesis | Gulliver et al., 2010 [2] | Describe findings as young-people participants’ reported patterns, not causes for a viewer. |
| Support and encouragement can facilitate help-seeking. | Systematic-review synthesis | Gulliver et al., 2010 [2] | Offer a possible first step; do not presume any relationship is safe. |
| Immediate safety danger calls for immediate local emergency/crisis support. | Public-health safety guidance | NIMH, revised 2025 [1] | General local wording only; no country-specific hotline or individualized triage. |

## Exclusions

The reel does not diagnose a mental-health condition, prescribe therapy/medication, promise benefit, infer intent or risk, present self-help as care, or claim that every person should disclose to family/friends. It does not use numerical prevalence, an individual causal explanation, or a global service recommendation.
