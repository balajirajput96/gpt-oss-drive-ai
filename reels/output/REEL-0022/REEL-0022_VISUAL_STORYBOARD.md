# Reel 0022 Text-Free Visual Storyboard

| Scene | Time | Text-free original motion-graphic concept | Ethical boundary |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 0–9s | A calm human silhouette guides a single warm beam through an abstract field of floating signals. | Attention is represented symbolically; no brand, app UI, or person imitation. |
| 2 | 9–18s | A clear glass prism reveals the path of a glowing signal toward an open doorway. | Visual metaphor for disclosed purpose, not a claim about any platform. |
| 3 | 18–28s | A branching pathway with one gently lit route and several equally open alternatives. | Metaphor for meaningful options; no legal compliance claim. |
| 4 | 28–37s | A balanced mobile holds a small personal compass on one side and anonymous activity particles on the other. | Illustrates competing values without asserting intent or wrongdoing. |
| 5 | 37–47s | Four soft geometric tokens orbit an open hand: shield, lens, choice node, and connection point. | Represents privacy, transparency, choice, and consent abstractly. |
| 6 | 47–58s | The silhouette pauses, selects one beam, and the other signals fade into an ordered constellation. | Reflection and agency; no promise of outcomes. |

All scenes are original deterministic SVG-to-PNG motion graphics. They must remain free of text because Hindi captions are composited separately.
