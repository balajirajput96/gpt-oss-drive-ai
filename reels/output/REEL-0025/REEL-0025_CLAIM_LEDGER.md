# Reel 0025 Claim Ledger

| Narration proposition | Classification | Permitted wording | Explicit boundary | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Buddhist meditation traditions are not homogeneous. | Scholarly review of traditions | “बौद्ध परंपराएँ एक जैसी नहीं हैं।” | Do not identify a single authoritative Buddhist view. | Amihai & Kozhevnikov, 2015 [1] |
| Some Theravada accounts describe shamatha as stabilizing/steadying the mind. | Traditional description in scholarly review | “कुछ थेरवाद वर्णनों में shamatha… मन को स्थिर करने की साधना…” | Not a treatment or a promised emotional result. | Amihai & Kozhevnikov, 2015 [1] |

| Contemporary mindfulness and Buddhist `sati` should not automatically be equated. | Scholarly opinion paper | “अपने-आप एक ही मान लेना सही नहीं।” | Do not demean secular practice or decide authenticity. | Thupten, 2019 [2] |

## Exclusions

The production excludes therapeutic efficacy claims, brain or autonomic-system claims, enlightenment claims treated as fact, conversion messaging, traditions ranked as superior, and any statement that attention training guarantees calm, morality, wisdom, or compassion.
