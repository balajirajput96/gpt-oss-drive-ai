# Reel 0025 Text-Free Visual Storyboard

**Visual route:** Original deterministic SVG motion-graphics frames, rendered to six 720×1280 transparent-text-free PNGs. Hindi captions are composited separately by the renderer.

| Scene | Time intent | Text-free visual concept | Narrative function |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 01 | 0–9 s | A single warm attention beam meets three differently shaped, nonreligious paths around an observing silhouette. | Establishes the hook: attention is not one simple idea. |
| 02 | 9–18 s | Three distinct orbit systems arrange around a quiet central field, with no path visually dominating. | Shows non-homogeneity across traditions. |
| 03 | 18–28 s | A hand-free bowl-like vessel holds concentric settling ripples and a poised focal point. | Abstractly supports the textual/traditional `shamatha` description without promising an outcome. |
| 04 | 28–39 s | Three interdependent geometric rings and a compass-like structure balance around a human-scale figure. | Represents the attributed ethics, concentration, and wisdom context without words or sacred iconography. |
| 05 | 39–50 s | Two overlapping but nonidentical circles connect by a narrow luminous bridge amid distinct patterned fields. | Illustrates possible overlap yet non-equivalence between modern mindfulness and Buddhist `sati`. |
| 06 | 50–61 s | A question-shaped open loop, directional stars, and an inward-facing silhouette invite reflection on intention and context. | Closes with a non-prescriptive question. |

## Visual provenance requirements

All six visuals must be generated only by `reels/scripts/generate-reel0025-visuals.mjs`. They contain no embedded text, generated logos, portraits, sacred-symbol claims, or reused legacy image data. The provenance record must state `imageGenerationUsed:false`, `quotaOrAccessControlBypass:false`, `reusedLegacyAsset:false`, `embeddedText:false`, and `realPersonDepicted:false`.
