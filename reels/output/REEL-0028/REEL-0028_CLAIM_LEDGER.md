# Reel 0028 Claim Ledger

| Script idea | Label | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Reports, behaviour, and brain signals are different evidence streams. | Empirical-methodological framing | Do not claim a measure is meaningless or sufficient on its own. [1] [3] |
| A direct external ground truth for another person’s lived experience is missing. | Measurement limitation | “Ground truth” is a careful shorthand for a directly independently validated experience readout, not a claim that experience is inaccessible to research. [1] [2] |
| Reports can involve wording, memory, and decision factors. | Measurement limitation | Do not say every self-report is inaccurate. [1] [2] |
| Performance/behaviour is indirect evidence of experience. | Empirical-methodological framing | Do not infer absence of experience from one correct, incorrect, absent, or present behavioural response. [1] [4] |
| Neural patterns need interpretation and validation against experience evidence. | Open research question | Do not claim a scan reads every thought or diagnoses consciousness in an individual. [3] [4] |
| Combining streams can make the inference more informative. | Scientific humility | No guarantee, medical recommendation, or settled theory claim. [1] [3] |

Excluded: clinical diagnosis or prognosis; treatment advice; individual consciousness verdict; mind-reading claim; one universal brain signature; numerical accuracy claim; claim that consciousness research is impossible; claim that neuroscience proves or disproves spirituality, dualism, physicalism, or any other metaphysical position.
