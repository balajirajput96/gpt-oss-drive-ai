# Reel 0016 Claim Ledger

| Time | Spoken claim | Evidence label | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–10s | A small, real success can provide task-specific information. | Theory/review | It is not proof of broad ability or a guaranteed future outcome.[^1] |
| 10–22s | Mastery experiences are described as information for self-efficacy beliefs. | Theory/review | Effects depend on context and the person’s interpretation.[^1] |
| 22–36s | Clear, achievable proximal goals and honest feedback can help people evaluate progress. | Educational implication | Empty praise is not a substitute for feedback.[^1] |
| 36–50s | Intervention results can differ across settings and studies. | Systematic-review limitation | A health-education review is not a universal motivation result.[^2] |
| 50–60s | Ask what worked, what the next task is, and what evidence would change the plan. | Practical inference | Not medical, clinical, or guaranteed behavior advice. |

[^1]: Artino, A. R. (2012). https://doi.org/10.1007/s40037-012-0012-5
[^2]: Busabong, W. et al. (2025). https://doi.org/10.3347/PHD.24074
