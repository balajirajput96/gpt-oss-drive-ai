# Reel 0015 Claim Ledger

| Time | Spoken claim | Evidence label | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–10s | A result can fit more than one explanation. | Methodological principle | Not a claim that any specific study is wrong. |
| 10–23s | Attention tasks can overlap with memory, response selection and decision demands. | Review/theory | Conceptual overlap is not proof of a cause in an individual result.[^1] |
| 23–38s | Attention and decision processes can be experimentally confounded unless the design separates them. | Empirical/methodological finding | A particular two-stage study demonstrated separation; designs differ.[^2] |
| 38–52s | Causal claims need suitable manipulation and controls for alternatives. | Methodological analysis | Correlation alone does not establish causation.[^3] |
| 52–60s | Name the measure, group, comparison, and plausible alternatives. | Research-practice inference | Not diagnostic or medical advice. |

[^1]: Oberauer, K. (2019). https://doi.org/10.5334/joc.58
[^2]: Moerel, D. et al. (2024). https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0224-24.2024
[^3]: Dijkstra, N., & de Bruin, L. (2016). https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyt.2016.00129
