# Reel 0031 Text-free Visual Storyboard

## Production brief

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Explain selective priority without reducing attention to a “focus trick.” |
| Medium | Six original 720×1280 portrait source PNGs for a Hindi educational reel. |
| Visual language | Deterministic abstract scientific motion-graphic scenes: deep ink background, violet-blue signal fields, amber priority pathways, and mint contrast details. |
| Text | None. Captions are composited only by the final renderer. |
| People | None; no real person or character depiction. |
| Asset route | New per-reel SVG source rendered to PNG. Image-model generation is not used because access quota is exhausted; this is not a bypass. |

## Scene plan

| Asset | Narration role | Original text-free composition | Transition intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| `r0031-attention-01-priority-field.png` | Hook: attention is not just long concentration | A crowded field of translucent circles; one amber orbit coheres around a single moving point. | Establishes competing information and changing priority. |
| `r0031-attention-02-selection-lens.png` | Family of selection processes | A large glass-like oval lens reveals a crisp patterned channel through diffuse visual noise. | Moves from misconception to working description. |
| `r0031-attention-03-location-feature-rule.png` | Location, feature/object, and task-rule examples | Three distinct tracks—spatial grid, shape constellation, and rule-like branching path—converge on one central selector. | Shows multiple possible targets without textual labels. |
| `r0031-attention-04-salience-goal.png` | Salience and goals both matter | A bright violet flare and a steady amber beacon exert different visual pulls on a flowing particle stream. | Contrasts automatic capture with goal-guided selection. |
| `r0031-attention-05-related-not-identical.png` | Alertness, orienting, and control are related but not identical | Three differently shaped rhythmic signal systems overlap but retain separate looping paths. | Communicates relation without identity. |
| `r0031-attention-06-awareness-boundary.png` | Awareness distinction and takeaway | An open halo and a narrow spotlight coexist over a calm field of dim shapes, ending with a balanced priority constellation. | Holds complexity, then closes on the selective-priority takeaway. |
